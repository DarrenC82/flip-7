# Flip 7 — Official Rules  
**Edition 3.1 – The Op Games (2025)**  
Ages 8+ | 3+ Players | 20 Minutes  

---

## OBJECTIVE

In this press-your-luck game, players compete to earn the most points to win.  
You score points based on the total number value of the cards in front of you.  

The more valuable a card is, the more copies of that card there are in the deck.  

If you can successfully Flip 7 unique Number cards into your line, you automatically end the round for everyone and score **15 bonus points**.  

However, if you ever draw a second card with the same number as one already in your line, you **bust** and are out of the round, scoring nothing.

## HOW TO PLAY

1. Have pen and paper ready to keep track of scores.  
   (You can also use the printable scoresheets, videos, or scoring web app via QR code.)

2. Shuffle the deck thoroughly and choose one player to be the **Dealer** for the round.

3. The Dealer deals one card face up to each player, including themselves.  
   - If an **Action card** appears, pause dealing and resolve it immediately before continuing.

4. Not every player will necessarily have one Number card at this stage. Some may have multiple cards depending on what Action cards were dealt.

---

## PLAYER TURNS

In turn order, the Dealer offers each player the option to:

- **“Hit”** – get dealt another card  
- **“Stay”** – exit the round and bank your points

If you Hit, place the Number cards in a single visible line.  
You may Stay as long as you have at least one card in front of you.

---

### Example
After 4 rounds, a player has chosen to Hit four times.  
They have three Number cards and one Score Modifier card.  
If they choose to Stay next, they will end the round with **32 points**.

**Number Card Total:** 11 + 5 + 12 = 28  
**+4 Bonus Modifier:** 28 + 4 = 32 Points

---

## REMEMBER
The only way to achieve the **Flip 7 Bonus** is by having **seven unique Number cards** face up in front of you.  
Duplicate numbers cause a **bust** and eliminate you from the round.

---

## ACTION CARDS

Action cards can be played on any active
player including yourself. If you are the only
active player in the round, you must play
the Action card on yourself. Set these cards
above your Number cards.

### FREEZE!
The player receiving this card **banks all the points they have collected** and is out of the round immediately.

---

### FLIP THREE!
The player who receives this card must accept the next **three cards**, flipping them one at a time.

- Stop if the player either flips 7 unique Number cards or busts.  
- If a **Second Chance** card appears, it may be set aside and used later.  
- If another Flip Three or Freeze card appears, resolve it **after** all three cards are flipped.

---

### SECOND CHANCE!
Keep this card. If the player with this card is later dealt a duplicate number, they discard **Second Chance** and the duplicate number card — avoiding a bust.

Rules for Second Chance:
- You may only have **one** Second Chance card at a time.  
- If dealt another, give it to another active player.  
- If no one else is active or all have one, discard it.  
- All Second Chance cards are discarded at the end of each round, even if unused.

---

## SCORE MODIFIER CARDS

Modifier cards do not count toward achieving a Flip 7.  
They can be played alongside Number cards to adjust your score.

### +2 to +10 Cards
Add the shown amount to the sum of your Number cards.

### x2 Card
Double the total points for all your Number cards.  
(First multiply your Number total, then add any +X bonuses.)

---

## END OF A ROUND

A round ends when **either**:

1. There are no active players remaining (everyone has either busted or chosen to stay), **or**  
2. A player flips **7 unique Number cards**, ending the round immediately.

Players who bust or freeze flip their cards face down until the round ends.

---

## SCORING SUMMARY

1. Add the values of all **Number Cards**.  
2. Apply any **Modifier Cards** (+2, +4, etc., or ×2).  
3. Add any additional bonus points.  
4. If you flipped 7 unique Number Cards, add **+15 bonus points**.  

Players who bust score **0**.

---

## STARTING THE NEXT ROUND

- Set all cards from the round aside (do **not** reshuffle yet).  
- Pass the remaining deck to the left; that player becomes the new Dealer.  
- When the deck runs out, shuffle the discard pile to form a new deck.  
  (If you need to reshuffle mid-round, leave all active players’ cards as they are.)

---

## END OF THE GAME

At the end of the round when **at least one player reaches 100 points** (or the chosen winning score), the player with the most points wins.

> **Optional Challenge:**  
> Playing solo or with two players? Try to score **200 points in under 5 rounds!**
